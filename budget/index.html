Union Budget 2026-27

Where the money comes from, and where it goes

The Union government plans to spend ₹53.5 lakh crore in 2026-27. It will borrow ₹17 lakh crore of that, 31.7% of the total. Interest on past borrowing takes ₹14 lakh crore, 26.3%. By Sep 2026, 53.6% of the year's capital budget had been spent.
