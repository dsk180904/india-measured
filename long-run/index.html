Long run

Four centuries in one line

In 1820 India produced 16.7% of the world's output. By 1980 that had fallen to 3.2%. It is now 8.1% and rising. Income per person at independence in 1947 was lower than the estimate for 1600.

In 1600 the average Briton was 1.3 times as rich as the average Indian. At the widest the gap was 15 times; it is now 4.9

How far to trust this

These are reconstructions by economic historians, coordinated by the Maddison Project at the University of Groningen. Figures for the twentieth century rest on national accounts. Figures before about 1870 rest on wages, prices, tax records and a good deal of judgement, and carry wide margins of error. Read the early numbers for their order of magnitude and their direction.

Borders changed. Estimates for India before 1947 cover a different territory from the country today. Dollar values are in 2011 international dollars, which adjust for differences in prices between countries and over time, so they are not comparable with the current-dollar figures elsewhere on this site.

World totals exist only for benchmark years, which is why the share chart has few points before 1950.
