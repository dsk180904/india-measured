What this is, and what it leaves out

The activity index is a plain average. For each component, the latest year-on-year growth rate is compared with that component's own average and spread since 2012, giving a number of standard deviations from normal. The index is the mean of those numbers for every component that has reported, provided at least three have. It is experimental, it is not a forecast of GDP, and it will be revised whenever its inputs are.

Three series that analysts usually watch are missing because their publishers block automated collection: UPI payments, GST collections and e-way bills. Fuel sales begin only in April 2023 in the public data, so they enter the index from April 2024.
