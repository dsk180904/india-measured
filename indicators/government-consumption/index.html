About this measure

General government final consumption expenditure as a share of GDP.

The World Bank defines it this way: Final consumption expenditure is expenditure on goods and services by resident institutional units for the direct satisfaction of human needs or wants, whether individual or collective. General government FCE includes all government current expenditures for purchases of goods and services (including compensation of employees), and most expenditures on national defense and security, but excludes government military expenditures that are part of government capital formation. This indicator is expressed as a percentage of Gross Domestic Product (GDP) which is the total income earned through the production of goods and services in an economic territory during an accounting period.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
