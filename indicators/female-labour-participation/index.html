About this measure

Share of women aged 15 and over who are working or looking for work. Modelled ILO estimate.

The World Bank defines it this way: The labor force participation rate is the labor force as a percent of the population ages 15 and older. The labor force is the sum of all persons of working age who are employed and those who are unemployed.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
