About this measure

Passengers carried by airlines registered in the country, domestic and international.

The World Bank defines it this way: Air carrier data per country refers to passengers carried by airlines registered in that country regardless of the origin or destination of the passengers.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
