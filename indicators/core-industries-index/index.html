About this measure

Index of Eight Core Industries, the combined output of coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilisers, steel, cement and electricity, with 2022-23 as 100.

Base 2022-23 = 100, so the series begins in April 2023; it is not linked to the 2011-12 base. Not seasonally adjusted. The latest months are provisional and are revised.
