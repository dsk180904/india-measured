About this measure

Freight handled at Indian airports in the month, domestic and international together, in thousand tonnes.

Scheduled commercial traffic at all operational airports, counted at each airport, so a domestic passenger is counted once on departure and once on arrival. Each report states a month and the same month a year earlier; the figure from the later report is used.
