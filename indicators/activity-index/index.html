About this measure

How far monthly activity is from its own normal, averaged across industrial output, goods trade, bank credit and fuel sales. Zero is the average pace; plus one is one standard deviation above it.

Experimental, calculated on this site. Each component's year-on-year growth is measured against its own average and spread since 2012, leaving out the pandemic months, and the results are averaged. A month is shown when at least three components have reported. A component joins once it has two years of history. Components now: 6.
