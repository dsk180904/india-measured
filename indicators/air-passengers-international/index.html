About this measure

International passengers handled at Indian airports in the month.

Scheduled commercial traffic at all operational airports, counted at each airport, so a domestic passenger is counted once on departure and once on arrival. Each report states a month and the same month a year earlier; the figure from the later report is used. Published in millions to two decimal places.
