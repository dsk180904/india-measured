About this measure

Share of people aged 15 and over who are working or looking for work. Modelled ILO estimate.

The World Bank defines it this way: Labor force participation rate is the proportion of the population ages 15 and older that is economically active: all people who supply labor for the production of goods and services during a specified period.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
