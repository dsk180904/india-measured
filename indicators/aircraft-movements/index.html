About this measure

Take-offs and landings by scheduled commercial flights at Indian airports in the month, in thousands.

Scheduled commercial traffic at all operational airports, counted at each airport, so a domestic passenger is counted once on departure and once on arrival. Each report states a month and the same month a year earlier; the figure from the later report is used. General aviation is left out.
