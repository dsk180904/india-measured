About this measure

Route length of Indian Railways at the end of the fiscal year, in thousand kilometres.

Fiscal years, April to March, filed under the year they begin. The latest year is provisional. Freight figures leave out the Konkan Railway. From the railway ministry’s annual statements as reprinted in the Economic Survey, which is updated once a year; the ministry’s monthly figures are not available to a script.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
