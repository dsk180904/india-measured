About this measure

Assessable value of the goods covered by e-way bills generated in the month, in rupees crore.

An e-way bill is the electronic permit needed to move a consignment worth more than ₹50,000, so the count tracks goods moving by road and rail. Added up on this site from the state tables: bills for movement within a state plus bills for goods leaving it.
