About this measure

Renewable energy as a share of total final energy consumption.

The World Bank defines it this way: Renewable energy consumption is the share of renewable energy in total final energy consumption.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
