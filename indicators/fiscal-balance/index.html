About this measure

General government net lending or borrowing as a share of GDP. Negative is a deficit.

IMF series: Net lending (+) / net borrowing (-), General government, Percent of GDP

Figures from 2025 are IMF staff projections, published 2026-10-13.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
