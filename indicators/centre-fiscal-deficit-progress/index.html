About this measure

The Union fiscal deficit from April to the month shown, as a share of the full year's budgeted deficit.

Calculated on this site. Measured against the Budget Estimate published with each month’s accounts. The share can pass 100 or fall during the year because receipts and spending do not arrive evenly.
