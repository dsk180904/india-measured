About this measure

The country's share of world GDP at purchasing power parity, with IMF staff projections.

IMF series: Gross domestic product (GDP), Purchasing power parity (PPP) international dollar, percent of world GDP, Percent, ICP benchmarks 2017-2021

Figures from 2026 are IMF staff projections, published 2026-10-13.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
