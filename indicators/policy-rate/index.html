About this measure

The Reserve Bank of India's policy rate at the end of each month: the repo rate from 2001, the Bank Rate before that.

From 3 Apr 2001 onwards: official repo overnight rate; from 1 Jan 1946 to 2 Apr 2001: oank rate.
