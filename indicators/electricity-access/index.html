About this measure

Share of the population with access to electricity.

The World Bank defines it this way: Access to electricity is the percentage of population with access to electricity. Electrification data are collected from industry, national surveys and international sources.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
