About this measure

Unemployment among people aged 15 to 24. Modelled ILO estimate.

The World Bank defines it this way: Youth unemployment refers to the share of the labor force ages 15-24 without work but available for and seeking employment.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
