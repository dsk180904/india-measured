About this measure

Length of national highways at the end of the fiscal year, in thousand kilometres.

Fiscal years, April to March, filed under the year they begin. From the road transport ministry’s statistics as reprinted in the Economic Survey. The table gives 1990-91, 2000-01 and every year from 2011-12; the newest years are provisional. The 2025-26 figure is a part-year reading (upto December) from the Survey’s infrastructure chapter.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
