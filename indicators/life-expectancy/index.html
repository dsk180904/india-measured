About this measure

Years a newborn would live if current mortality patterns stayed the same.

The World Bank defines it this way: Life expectancy at birth indicates the number of years a newborn infant would live if prevailing patterns of mortality at the time of its birth were to stay the same throughout its life.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
