About this measure

Capital expenditure of the Union government in the month, in rupees crore: roads, railways, defence equipment and loans to states for investment.

Unaudited provisional accounts. Monthly figures are calculated on this site as the change in the published year-to-date total, so a correction to an earlier month shows up in the month it is made. March figures are provisional until the accounts close.
