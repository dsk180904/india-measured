About this measure

Share of the labour force without work but available and looking. Modelled ILO estimate.

The World Bank defines it this way: Unemployment refers to the share of the labor force that is without work but available for and seeking employment.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
