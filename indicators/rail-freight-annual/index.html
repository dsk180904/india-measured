About this measure

Goods carried by rail, in million tonne-kilometres.

The World Bank defines it this way: Goods transported by railway are the volume of goods transported by railway, measured in metric tons times kilometers traveled.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
