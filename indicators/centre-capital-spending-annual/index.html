About this measure

Capital expenditure of the Union government in the fiscal year, in rupees crore.

Fiscal years, April to March, filed under the year they begin. Provisional accounts for April to March as published by the Controller General of Accounts; audited figures in later Budgets can differ slightly.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
