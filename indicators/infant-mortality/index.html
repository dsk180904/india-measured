About this measure

Deaths before age one per 1,000 live births.

The World Bank defines it this way: Infant mortality rate is the number of infants dying before reaching one year of age, per 1,000 live births in a given year.

India's national accounts follow its fiscal year, April to March. International sources file each fiscal year under the calendar year in which it begins, so the figure for 2024 covers April 2024 to March 2025.
