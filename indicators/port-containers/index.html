About this measure

Containers handled at the twelve major ports in the month, in thousand twenty-foot equivalent units (TEU).

The twelve major ports run by the central government only; the non-major ports, which handle a little under half of India's cargo, are reported separately and not included. The latest month is an advance estimate and the year before it is provisional. Each report states a month and the same month a year earlier; the figure from the later report is used. Months for which the ministry's listing has no report are missing.
