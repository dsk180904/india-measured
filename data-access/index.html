Data access

Into your spreadsheet or your code

Annual series are plain files at stable addresses, open to anyone. Monthly, weekly and daily series, the revision log and the nowcast come through the keyed API on Pro. Either way, point a spreadsheet or a script at an address once and it stays current.

Get an API keyFree and Pro compared

In a spreadsheet

Google Sheets

Paste into any cell. It refreshes on its own.

=IMPORTDATA("ORIGIN/data/series/gdp-growth.csv")

Swap gdp-growth for any indicator id. The id is the last part of an indicator page's address.

Excel

The keyed API

Send your key as a bearer token, or as ?key= where a header is awkward, such as a spreadsheet formula. Free keys allow 200 requests a day, Pro keys 20,000. Each response carries X-RateLimit-Remaining.

From the command line

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \n  "ORIGIN/api/v1/series/power-demand-daily?from=2026"

Google Sheets, with a key

=IMPORTDATA("ORIGIN/api/v1/series/cpi-inflation-monthly.csv?key=YOUR_KEY")
EndpointReturnsPlan
/api/v1/catalogEvery series with its unit, frequency, coverage, latest value and whether your plan can read itFree
/api/v1/series/{id}One series as JSON. Add .csv for CSV, and ?from=2015&to=2026 to limit the periodFree for annual, Pro for the rest
/api/v1/series?ids=a,b,cUp to 50 series in one requestFree for annual, Pro for the rest
/api/v1/releases?since=2026-11-01Every new figure and revision picked up since a datePro
/api/v1/vintages/{id}The revision history of one series: what was first reported and what it was changed toPro
/api/v1/nowcastThe current GDP nowcast, its inputs and its track recordPro

Errors come back as JSON with an error code and a message: 401 for a missing or revoked key, 402 when the series needs Pro, 429 when the day's allowance is used up.

Periods are written as 2024 for a year, 2025-Q2 for a calendar quarter and 2025-07 for a month. Annual figures follow India's fiscal year, filed under the year it begins. Ids are listed in the catalogue and on the indicators page.

Terms

The figures belong to their publishers: among them the World Bank, the IMF, India's statistics ministry, the Reserve Bank, the finance ministry, the GST Network, the BIS and the Maddison Project. Each series names its source. Credit the original source when you publish them. Files change when sources revise their data; the releases page records each change.
