How fast, how big

India's economy has shrunk in only 5 of the 65 years on record. The pace changed after the 1991 reforms.

Structure

What the economy is made of

India moved from farms to services without the long factory boom that East Asia went through. Work has shifted more slowly than output: 41.6% of workers are still in agriculture, which produces 16.2% of GDP.

Services have been larger than agriculture every year since 1981

India in the world

Large in total, modest per person

India is one of the largest economies in total. Per person, its income is 46% of the world average. The IMF projects it will be the 3rd-largest economy by 2031.
