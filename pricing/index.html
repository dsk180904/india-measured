Questions

How do I pay? By card or UPI through Razorpay, monthly. You can cancel at any time from your account page and keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

Whose data is it? The figures belong to their publishers: the World Bank, the IMF, India's statistics ministry, the Reserve Bank, Grid-India, the OECD, the BIS and others. Pro pays for collection, checking, the revision record and delivery. Credit the original source when you publish.

What is not included? UPI payments, GST collections and e-way bills are not on the site yet. The nowcast and activity index are experimental and their track records are published beside them.

A team or a redistribution licence? Write to us from your account email and say what you need.
