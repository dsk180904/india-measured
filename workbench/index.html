Change on a year earlier is the percentage change from the same period a year before. For series already in per cent, such as interest rates and shares of GDP, it is the change in percentage points.

Index sets every series to 100 at its first value in the window, which is how to compare series in different units. Moving average smooths over a year: twelve months, four quarters, or three years for annual data.

Series in different units cannot share an axis as published. The workbench converts them to a common basis, growth rates or an index, and says which. It never draws two scales on one chart.
